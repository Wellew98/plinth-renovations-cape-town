# SEO basics: plinthrenovations.co.za

> Written 2026-11-11, 08:58 SAST. Scope: the SEO basics only, for a site with **no
> Google Business Profile**. Nothing is changed until this plan is approved.
> Every number below was measured today, not remembered.

**The one-line answer.** The on-page basics are done and pass our own gate (34/34 pages,
0 problems). What is missing is not more optimisation. It is that **Google has not
indexed this site**: 8 URLs are in the index and 7 of those are legacy URLs that now
return 301. The 34 real pages are not in it. Second, there are **zero external links**
pointing at the domain. Until those two are fixed, nothing else we do can produce
traffic.

## 2. The diagnosis, in order of what is actually costing traffic

### 2.1 Blocker: Google's index holds the old site, not this one

Search Console reports **8 indexed, 10 not indexed**. The eight indexed URLs are:

| Indexed URL | Last crawled | Reality today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `https://plinthrenovations.co.za/` | 3 Nov 2026 | live, current |
| `/estimate.html` | 5 Nov 2026 | 301 to `/estimate` |
| `/projects.html` | 3 Nov 2026 | 301 to `/our-work` |
| `/areas.html` | 3 Nov 2026 | 301 to `/service-areas` |
| `/gallery.html` | 3 Nov 2026 | 301 to `/our-work` |
| `/blog.html` | 3 Nov 2026 | 301 to `/faq` |
| `/faq.html` | 3 Nov 2026 | 301 to `/faq` |
| `/about.html` | 3 Nov 2026 | 301 to `/about` |

So one URL of the current site is confirmed indexed. The fourteen compact pages, seven
coverage pages, three hubs, `/services`, `/solutions`, `/our-work`, `/contact` and the
rest are **not in the index at all**. Search Console will not list them under a reason
either, because the Pages report only counts URLs Google knows about.

The cause is timing, not markup. Google crawled on 3 November. The slug renames, the
sitemap rebuild and the redirects landed 9 and 10 November. Google has not been back.
The 10 "not indexed" split as `Alternative page with proper canonical tag` (7),
`Page with redirect` (2) and `Discovered, currently not indexed` (1), which is the
normal residue of a mid-flight URL change, not a fault to fix.

The sitemap is not the problem: submitted 31 October, **last read 10 November, status
Success, 34 discovered pages**. Google knows the URLs exist. It has not chosen to crawl
and index them yet, and on a 11-day-old domain with no links, it will not hurry.

### 2.2 Blocker: zero external links

Search Console, Links report: **External links, Total 0.** No linking sites, no top
linking pages, no anchor text. This is the binding constraint on ranking. Google's
basics are not "optimise harder", they are "be crawlable, be useful, be linked". Two of
three are done.

The internal-link figure in that same report is stale, and it is a clue: it lists
`/projects.html` (6 inlinks), `/areas.html` (5), `/gallery.html` (4), `/faq.html` (2),
`/about.html` (1). Those are the old site's own links, from the 3 November crawl.

Without a Google Business Profile the usual local shortcut is gone: no map pack, no
"near me" proximity ranking, no review signal. Everything has to come from organic
ranking and citations, and both need links.

### 2.3 Real defect: 288 internal links point at redirects

Every one of the 36 pages carries a footer with eight service links. All eight point at
URLs that were renamed on 9 November and now 301:

| Footer says (301) | Should say (200) |
| --- | --- |
| `/waterproofing` | `/waterproofing-cape-town` |
| `/damp-proofing` | `/waterproofing-cape-town` |
| `/painting` | `/painters-cape-town` |
| `/kitchen-renovations` | `/kitchen-renovations-cape-town` |
| `/bathroom-renovations` | `/bathroom-renovations-cape-town` |
| `/home-renovations` | `/home-renovations-cape-town` |
| `/plastering` | `/plastering-cape-town` |
| `/home-extensions` | `/home-renovations-cape-town` |

36 pages x 8 links = **288 internal links that resolve through a 301**. Two
consequences. Google sees a site whose own navigation still describes the old
structure, on a domain it is deciding whether to crawl, which is exactly the signal
that keeps crawl priority low. And four of the strongest compact pages,
`/roof-waterproofing-cape-town`, `/roof-repairs-cape-town`, `/tiling-cape-town` and
`/leaking-ceiling-repair`, get **no footer link at all**, so they are reachable only
through the hubs.

Source of the bug: `src/ui.py`, `footer()`, lines 225 to 234. It was never updated when
the slugs changed. `seo_check.py` does not catch it because its link graph normalises
`.html` but does not resolve `_redirects`.

### 2.4 Smaller on-page gaps

Measured, ranked by value:

1. **151 images, 0 with `width` or `height`.** All lazy-loaded, none with intrinsic
   size. `extra.css` carries 4 `aspect-ratio` rules, not enough to cover 151 images.
   This is the one plausible Core Web Vitals defect and it is cheap to fix.
2. **No WebP or `srcset`.** Images total 8.0 MB across the site, largest single file
   356 KB. Service-page images run 130 to 356 KB at one size for every screen.
3. **`/thank-you` is indexable.** It is not in the sitemap and has no inbound link, but
   it has no `noindex` either. Only `404.html` carries `noindex` today.
4. **Seven coverage pages carry only `BreadcrumbList`.** No `LocalBusiness` reference, no
   `areaServed`, no `Service`. The fourteen compact pages are complete.
5. **Eight titles are thin or geo-less:** `/solutions` (30 chars), `/accessibility` (34),
   `/terms` (33), `/disclaimer` (31), `/thank-you` (30), `/tiling-cape-town` (37),
   `/our-work` (40), `/faq` (45). Titles are unique and none exceeds 62 characters.
6. **Sitemap has no `lastmod`.** 34 `<loc>` entries, 0 `<lastmod>`. For a new site asking
   Google to recrawl after a rebuild, this is the cheapest possible hint and we are not
   sending it.
7. **`plinth-renovations-cape-town.pages.dev` still serves 200.** Canonical points at the
   apex, so Google will consolidate, but a duplicate host that answers 200 on a site
   this young is avoidable noise.

### 2.5 What is already right, so we stop worrying about it

Stated plainly so it is not re-litigated later:

- All 34 sitemap URLs return 200. `http`, `www` and `http://www` all 301 to the apex
  over HTTPS. `.html` variants 308 to clean URLs. Unknown paths return a real 404.
- Every page self-canonicals to its `https://plinthrenovations.co.za/...` URL.
- One `h1` per page, keyword-led, aligned to title and slug. Titles and descriptions
  unique. Every image has descriptive alt text.
- Internal linking is hub and spoke, maximum depth 2, zero orphans.
- JSON-LD `HomeAndConstructionBusiness` with address, hours, `areaServed`, plus
  `Service`, `BreadcrumbList` and `FAQPage` where they belong.
- Content is differentiated, not merge-field clones: highest 6-word shingle overlap
  across all 35 pages is 22.8% (`/` against `/faq`), and the fourteen compact pages land
  400 to 500 words each.
- HTTPS with HSTS, `_headers` CSP, immutable caching on `/images/*`.
- `robots.txt` allows everything and declares the sitemap.

## 3. What "the basics" means for this site specifically

Google's own starter guide reduces to five duties: make the site crawlable, make each
page describable, make the content genuinely useful, make it fast enough, and earn the
links that tell Google it matters. This site has duties one, two and three done.

The reason it has no traffic is not a missing tactic. It is that duty one is only
half-true (Google has not crawled the current site) and duty five is not started
(zero links). A site with no links and one indexed page will return nothing no matter
how good the markup is.

With no Google Business Profile, two consequences follow and they shape the plan:

1. There is no map pack, so no proximity ranking and no review stars. Every visitor has
   to come from a blue link or a directory.
2. Local trust signals have to be built outside Google: Bing Places, SA directories,
   the Facebook page, supplier and partner pages. These are citations first and links
   second, and they are the only substitute for the profile.

## 5. Tier 1: the link problem (the actual growth work)

Zero external links is not a content problem and cannot be solved by editing HTML. It
needs a list, a sequence and his participation. This is the part of "basics" most sites
skip, and it is the part that decides whether the next 12 months produce anything.

Ordered by effort against return, all verified as real, none purchased:

1. **Bing Places for Business.** Free, no GBP needed, same NAP as the site. Bing also
   feeds some Windows and Copilot surfaces. This is the single closest substitute for
   the profile he does not have.
2. **NAP consistency pass.** One canonical form of name, address and phone used
   everywhere: `Plinth Renovations and Construction`, `64 Oxford Street, Richmond Estate,
   Goodwood, Cape Town, 7460`, `066 150 5770`. Any directory that disagrees gets fixed or
   skipped.
3. **SA directories that actually get crawled:** Yellow Pages South Africa, Brabys,
   Snupit, Cylex, Hotfrog, Yellosa, Bizcommunity. Claim the free listing, do not pay for
   placement. Ten of these is a real local citation footprint.
4. **The trade supply chain.** Every supplier Plinth buys from has a "find an installer"
   or "approved contractor" page, and most are free to join. That is where a building
   business gets its first genuinely relevant links. Needs his list of suppliers.
5. **The Facebook page** already linked in the footer. Make sure it names the website,
   the phone number and the service area in the same words the site uses.
6. **One local proof asset.** A single page nobody else has: for example the actual
   before/after record of two Cape Town jobs with the suburb, the problem, the method and
   the cost band. That is the page other people link to. It is also the page that best
   answers the "is this a real company" question that a missing profile leaves open.

What I will need from him: supplier names, whether he wants Bing Places done together,
and whether he has any existing directory listings to audit.

## 7. What I will not do

- No keyword stuffing, no doorway pages for suburbs with zero measured demand.
- No fake reviews, no invented project counts, no "serving 500+ homes".
- No purchased links, no private blog networks, no directory spam.
- No fake `LocalBusiness` markup pretending to a premises Plinth does not have.
- No second site or landing page network for the same business.
- No mass AI-generated suburb pages to inflate the URL count. One keyword, one page,
  and only where the demand was measured.
