Somerset West, as we find it

Somerset West sits under the Helderberg, and the slope decides a lot of what goes wrong. Water comes down the mountain and across large plots rather than sitting where it fell, so retaining walls, drainage and the level of the house relative to the ground matter as much as the roof does.

The housing stock is mixed: older homes in the town centre, large properties with pools and outbuildings, and newer estates. We travel from Goodwood for work here, so we plan it as fewer, longer visits rather than a daily run.

The Somerset West problems we get called for

These come up more often in this area than in the city as a whole.

  • Mountain runoff reaching the house because nothing slows it on the slope above.
  • Leaks where additions and outbuildings meet the original walls.
  • Damp at the base of walls where soil and paving hold water against them.
