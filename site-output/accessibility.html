Accessibility

We want this site to be usable by everyone, including people using a screen reader, a keyboard only, or larger text.

What we have done

The site uses real headings in order, descriptive alternative text on images, labelled form fields, visible focus states, and colour combinations chosen for contrast rather than fashion. The navigation works with a keyboard and the page does not depend on hover to reveal content.

What we know is not perfect

The site has not been through a formal WCAG audit. Some photographs of work in progress are low light and their descriptions are necessarily brief. The phone number is the primary contact and it is available on every page.

Tell us

If something on this site is difficult for you to use, call 066 150 5770 and tell us what happened. We will fix what we can and we will give you the information another way in the meantime.
