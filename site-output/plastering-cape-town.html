01Plastering

Plastering and plaster repair

Plastering in Cape Town means two different jobs: new plaster on a wall or ceiling, and repairing plaster that has failed. The second is more common. Hollow, blown or salt-damaged plaster usually has moisture behind it, and covering it only buys a season.

We cut back to sound material, treat the cause, then plaster to match the existing finish. Skim coats, crack repair, cornices and stucco Italiano are all done by the same team, which matters when the finish has to line up with a wall done twenty years ago.

How we work

Tap for hollow areas and check the wall for moisture.

Damp, leak or bridging dealt with before new plaster.

Applied, floated and finished ready for paint.
