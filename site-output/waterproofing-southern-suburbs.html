01Waterproofing Southern Suburbs

Waterproofing in the southern suburbs

Waterproofing in Cape Town's southern suburbs deals with older housing stock, steep sites and walls that hold water against them through winter. Cellars, under-house storage and boundary walls on a slope are common, and each fails differently.

We inspect, trace the water to its entry point, and choose between positive-side treatment, drainage and replastering depending on what is actually reachable. Where we cannot get to the wet side, we say so and design for the dry side instead.
