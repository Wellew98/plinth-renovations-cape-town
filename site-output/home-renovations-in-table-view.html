Coastal air changes what a renovation needs

Table View and the Bloubergstrand strip face the Atlantic, which means salt, wind and driving rain on the same wall. Metal corrodes faster, sealant fails sooner, and any fixing that was not specified for coastal conditions shows it within a couple of winters.

We see the results constantly. Balcony waterproofing that has lifted at the edge. Window frames with rust bleeding through the paint. Wall ties and lintels that have been quietly corroding behind the plaster. Roof sheets and fixings that have reached the end of their life on the sea side of the house while the land side still looks fine.

Coastal work is not about using a different product on the same detail. It is about specifying the right one, in the right sequence, and accepting that the west elevation will always need more attention than the east.
