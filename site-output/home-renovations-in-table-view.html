01The area

Table View, as we find it

Table View is coastal in a way that changes the specification: salt in the air, sand under the paving, wind that carries both. Flat roofs and balconies are common, and aluminium fittings, fixings and flashings take a beating that inland suburbs never see.

A lot of the housing here is also used differently from how it was built, with rooms let out or the house run as a holiday rental, so finishes need to survive turnover as well as weather. We choose materials for the coast rather than the catalogue default.

03What fails here

The Table View problems we get called for

These come up more often in this area than in the city as a whole.

  • Salt corrosion on fixings, flashings and aluminium frames, long before the roof itself fails.
  • Render eroding on the windward face, exposing the wall underneath.
  • Balcony waterproofing that was never detailed at the door threshold.
