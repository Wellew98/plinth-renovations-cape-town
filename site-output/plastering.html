Fix the plaster, or paint it twice

Plaster fails in Cape Town for reasons that are usually visible once you know what to look for. Movement cracks follow the line of a lintel or a joint between two materials. Hollow-sounding patches mean the plaster has lost its bond to the brick behind it. Powdering, salt-stained plaster at low level means moisture is coming through the wall, and no amount of skim will hold it.

We treat plaster as a repair job before a finishing job. Cracks are opened, not filled. Hollow plaster is cut back to sound material, not skimmed over. Where damp is the cause, the source is fixed before the wall is re-plastered, and where the wall needs to breathe we use a render that lets it.

For interior work, skimming an old wall flat is often the fastest way to make a room feel new without touching the structure. For exteriors, the priorities are different: weather resistance, crack control and a finish that can take the wind and the sun.

What we plaster

Interior and exterior, new work and repair, including the ceilings and mouldings that usually need attention at the same time.

Skimming

A thin, flat finish over sound plaster or plasterboard, ready for paint.

Crack repair

Cracks opened, reinforced where they are likely to move again, filled and finished flush.

Hollow and blown plaster

Unsound plaster cut back to a keyed surface and replaced, so the patch does not sound different from the wall.

Cornices and mouldings

Replacement and repair, with profiles matched to what is already in the house.

External plaster and render

Weather-facing repair and re-render, with the mix chosen for exposure and breathability.

Plasterboard ceilings

New ceilings, bulkheads and repairs, taped and skimmed ready for paint.

What changes the price of plastering

FactorWhy it moves the number
Repair or replaceSkimming a sound wall is quick. Cutting out and replacing blown plaster is not.
Height and accessDouble volumes and stairwells need scaffolding, which costs as much as the plastering.
What is on the wallOld enamel, wallpaper glue and multiple paint layers all have to be removed before new plaster will bond.
Matching existingCornice profiles and textured finishes take time to match, and sometimes a mould has to be made.
Drying timeNew plaster has to dry before paint. Rushing it is how you get a finish that fails in the first winter.

We price plastering per wall or per room after seeing the surface, and we tell you when plaster is not the real problem.

How we work

1. Assess

Tap for hollow areas, check crack patterns, and establish whether moisture is involved.

2. Prepare

Cut back unsound plaster, open cracks, remove failed coatings and key the surface.

3. Repair the cause

Where damp or movement is behind the failure, that is dealt with before new plaster goes on.

4. Plaster

Base coat and finish coat applied to the right thickness, with corners and reveals set true.

5. Finish ready for paint

Sanded, checked under light and handed over dry, so the painter is not correcting our work.
