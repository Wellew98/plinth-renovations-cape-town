01Tiling

Tiling floors, walls and wet areas

Tiling in Cape Town is mostly about what happens before the first tile is laid: screeding to a level, waterproofing a shower correctly, priming a surface that will take adhesive. Tiles laid on an uneven or wet substrate crack, lift or sound hollow within a year.

We screed, waterproof and tile with the same team, so nobody is blaming the previous trade. That matters most in bathrooms, where the waterproofing, the fall and the tile lines all have to agree.
