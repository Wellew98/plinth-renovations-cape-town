Complaints policy

If something is not right, tell us early. Almost every complaint we can fix is cheaper and faster to fix while we are still on site.

How to raise it

Call 066 150 5770, or write to us at 64 Oxford Street, Richmond Estate, Goodwood, Cape Town, 7460. Tell us the address, what is wrong and when you first noticed it. Photographs help.

What happens next

We will acknowledge your complaint and tell you who is handling it. We will inspect the work, and where the fault is ours we will put it right. Where the cause is outside our scope, we will explain what we found and what the correct repair is.

If we cannot agree

We will set out our position in writing, including what we inspected and what we found, so that you have a clear record to take further if you choose to.
