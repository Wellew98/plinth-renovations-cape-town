01Waterproofing Somerset West

Waterproofing in Somerset West

Waterproofing in Somerset West runs into older construction: slate and timber roofs, parapets without upstands, gutters sized for a different rainfall. Add wind off the Helderberg and water gets pushed into places a coating will not reach.

We inspect the roof, gutters and wall junctions, trace where the water actually enters, and specify a repair that suits the age of the building rather than covering it in a modern product that traps moisture.
