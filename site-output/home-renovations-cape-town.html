01Home Renovations

What a home renovation involves

Home renovations in Cape Town range from one room to a whole house, and the difference in cost is rarely the finishes. It is whether walls move, whether the electrical and plumbing need replacing, and how much of the house is live while the work happens.

We survey, check the structure rather than assume it, write a scope with sections priced separately, and phase the work so you can keep living in part of the house. Extensions, open-plan changes and full refurbishments all run on that sequence.
