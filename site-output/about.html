About Plinth Renovations and Construction

We are a renovation and construction company based at 64 Oxford Street in Richmond Estate, Goodwood, on the northern side of Cape Town. We work on houses and apartments within about 25 km of that base.

Why we start with the water

A renovation is a finish over a structure, and in Cape Town that structure spends four months a year being rained on sideways. We have seen too many bathrooms retiled over a failed membrane, and too many walls repainted over rising damp, to sell a finish without looking underneath it first.

So the first hour of a job is diagnosis. Where does the water enter? Is the damp rising, penetrating or condensing? Is the wall that is coming out carrying anything? Those answers decide the quote, and they decide whether the job lasts.
