About Plinth Renovations and Construction

Goodwood-based renovators serving Cape Town with honest scopes, fixed prices and tidy sites.

01Who we are

Built on vision, crafted with precision

Plinth started with a simple frustration: vague quotes, moving prices and messy sites. We do the opposite: written scope, fixed price, daily tidy, photo updates, and a handover you can walk through without a snag list.

Based at 64 Oxford Street, Richmond Estate, Goodwood, we specialise in the homes that make up most of Cape Town: facebrick and plaster 70s to 90s houses, townhouses and family homes that need real work done properly, not a patch that fails by the next winter.

  • Owner-supervised crews
  • Coastal-correct materials (stainless, treated timber, flexible waterproofing)
  • 12-month workmanship warranty on renovations
