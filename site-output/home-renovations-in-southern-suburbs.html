Southern Suburbs, as we find it

The southern suburbs hold the oldest housing we work on: Victorian and Edwardian homes in Claremont, Wynberg, Rondebosch and Newlands, with timber floors, high ceilings, slate or clay tile roofs, and often a cellar or a room below street level. Rain here comes off the mountain and sits longer than it does on the flats.

Work on these houses needs a lighter hand than a new build. Original details are worth keeping, and the building often has heritage considerations that decide what may change on the facade. We repair what can be repaired, and we are explicit about what cannot.

The Southern Suburbs problems we get called for

These come up more often in this area than in the city as a whole.

  • Valley and flashing leaks on pitched roofs after heavy winter rain.
  • Damp in cellars and rooms below street level where there is no barrier.
  • Paint failing on south-facing walls that never dry out between fronts.
