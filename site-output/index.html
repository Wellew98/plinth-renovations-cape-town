What we do, and how we start

Cape Town houses are built for wind and winter rain, and most of the problems we are called out to fix started years ago at a junction nobody looked at. A parapet that was never flashed properly. A balcony that drains towards the wall instead of away from it. Plaster straight onto brick with no cavity, holding water against the inside face until the paint blisters.

We renovate the whole house, but we start at the water. Before we quote a bathroom or a kitchen we look at the roof above it, the walls beside it and the falls on the floor. That is why our quotes sometimes include work you did not ask for, and why they hold up once the job is done.

We work on houses and apartments across Cape Town, from 1950s Goodwood semis to newer family homes in Durbanville and Table View. One team takes the job from strip-out to snag list, and you get the scope, the material list and the sequence in writing before anything is demolished.

A small Cape Town builder that starts with the water

Plinth Renovations and Construction is based in Goodwood, on the northern side of Cape Town. We are a small company by design. The person who quotes your job is on site while it runs, and the trades on your job are people we work with every week rather than whoever is free that Monday.

Most of our work comes from owners who have already had one bad experience. A leak that was painted over. A shower tiled without a fall to the drain. A quote that grew once the walls were open. So we do the unglamorous part first. We open the ceiling, read the moisture, and tell you what the water is doing before we sell you a finish.

We are also honest about scope. If a repair will hold, we say so instead of quoting a rebuild. If the waterproofing under your tiles has already failed, we tell you that new tiles will not save it.

What People Ask Us

How much does a renovation cost in Cape Town?

It depends on the scope, and anyone who quotes you a firm price per square metre before seeing the house is guessing. We visit, measure, open what needs opening, then give you one written estimate with the essential work and the optional work separated.

Do you handle waterproofing and damp as part of a renovation?

Yes, and we treat it as the first job rather than a line item at the end. A bathroom or kitchen quote that ignores the roof above it is a quote we would not sign off on ourselves.

How long does a bathroom or kitchen take?

We give you a date range in the written quote, based on the actual scope. We do not publish a standard number, because a bathroom that needs a drain moved is a different job from one that does not.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. A leaking parapet, one room painted, a ceiling patch, a single shower that has to be redone. Small jobs are often how we meet the owners we later build for.

Do you work outside Cape Town?

Stellenbosch and Somerset West by arrangement for larger jobs. Our normal working radius is about 25 km around Goodwood.

Do I need plans or council approval?

Structural work, alterations and additions usually need approval from the City of Cape Town before work starts. We tell you when it applies to your job, and we can work with your architect or draughtsman.

Can I stay in the house while you work?

Usually yes. We sheet off the work area, plan around the bathroom or kitchen you still need, and clean up at the end of each day.

How do payments work?

Staged against progress and set out in the quote before we start. We do not ask for the whole job upfront.
