02Why Plinth

Built for Cape Town. Not a Generic Contractor.

We are Plinth Renovations and Construction, based at 64 Oxford Street, Richmond Estate, Goodwood. After years working on homes across the Northern Suburbs and the greater city, we build for what most contractors underestimate: this climate.

At the foot of Table Mountain your home takes a unique beating. Atlantic salt air corrodes fixtures and fasteners. The summer Cape Doctor drives dust and UV into paint and timber. Winter brings sideways rain that finds every failed seal, flat roof and cracked parapet. Older Goodwood, Parow and Milnerton stock often hides damp, cracked plaster and tired electrics.

Our playbook is written for that: stainless or treated fixings near the coast, flexible waterproofing for parapets and flat roofs, mould-resistant prep, and honest advice on where to spend and where to save.

  • We diagnose before we quote: Moisture readings, a hose test and a look inside the ceiling before a number goes on paper.
  • One team, start to finish: The crew that strips your bathroom is the crew that waterproofs it, tiles it and hands it back.
  • Written scope and sequence: You know what is included, what is excluded and what order it happens in before demolition day.
  • We work in lived-in homes: Sheeting, daily clean-up and a plan for the kitchen or bathroom your family still has to use.

06FAQ

Cape Town Renovation FAQ

How much does a renovation cost in Cape Town? +

It depends on the scope, and anyone who quotes you a firm price per square metre before seeing the house is guessing. We visit, measure, open what needs opening, then give you one written estimate with the essential work and the optional work separated.

Do you handle waterproofing and damp as part of a renovation? +

Yes, and we treat it as the first job rather than a line item at the end. A bathroom or kitchen quote that ignores the roof above it is a quote we would not sign off on ourselves.

How long does a bathroom or kitchen take? +

We give you a date range in the written quote, based on the actual scope. We do not publish a standard number, because a bathroom that needs a drain moved is a different job from one that does not.

Do you take on small jobs? +

Yes. A leaking parapet, one room painted, a ceiling patch, a single shower that has to be redone. Small jobs are often how we meet the owners we later build for.

Do you work outside Cape Town? +

Stellenbosch and Somerset West by arrangement for larger jobs. Our normal working radius is about 25 km around Goodwood.

Do I need plans or council approval? +

Structural work, alterations and additions usually need approval from the City of Cape Town before work starts. We tell you when it applies to your job, and we can work with your architect or draughtsman.

Can I stay in the house while you work? +

Usually yes. We sheet off the work area, plan around the bathroom or kitchen you still need, and clean up at the end of each day.

How do payments work? +

Staged against progress and set out in the quote before we start. We do not ask for the whole job upfront.
