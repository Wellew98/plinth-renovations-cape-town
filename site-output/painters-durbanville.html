Painting in Durbanville

Painters in Durbanville work on walls that are chalky, cracked or holding damp that has been painted over before. Paint applied to a damp or unprepared wall fails within two winters, usually on the elevation that takes the weather.

We wash, scrape and repair first, treat damp at its source, then use a coating system matched to the surface. Interior work is done room by room with daily clean-up, so the house stays usable.
