Where we work

We are based in Goodwood and work within roughly 25 km of it. Below are the suburbs we write about in detail, because that is where most of our work is.

Durbanville

Older town-centre homes and newer estates, where north-westerly winter rain finds every weak junction.

Bellville

Dense 1960s and 1970s housing on plaster over brick, where damp shows first at skirting level.

Parow

Family homes and small commercial buildings, many of them due for their first proper waterproofing.

Table View

West-facing coastal homes where salt air, wind and balcony edges test every waterproofing detail.

Brackenfell

Family homes and additions, where a roofline that ties into the old house badly lets water straight in.

Southern Suburbs

Older Edwardian and Victorian homes in Rondebosch, Claremont, Wynberg and Plumstead, handled with care.
