Southern Suburbs

The southern suburbs hold the oldest housing we work on: Victorian and Edwardian homes in Claremont, Wynberg, Rondebosch and Newlands, with timber floors, high ceilings, slate or clay tile roofs, and often a cellar or a room below street level.
