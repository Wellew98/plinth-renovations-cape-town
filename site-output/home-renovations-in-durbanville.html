01The area

Durbanville, as we find it

Durbanville is two housing markets in one: the older town centre with houses from the 1950s onward on large plots, and the newer estates on the edges where the design rules decide what you may build before we do. Both are within easy reach of Goodwood.

The ground is the local factor. Clay soils move with the seasons, so cracks open and close in plaster and paving in a way that sandy northern suburbs do not see. That matters when a renovation adds load or when a wall has been patched repeatedly instead of repaired.

The Durbanville problems we get called for

These come up more often in this area than in the city as a whole.

  • Clay soil movement opening cracks in plaster and paving that reappear after filling.
  • Roof leaks at valleys and flashings on larger, steeper roofs.
  • Damp on walls that stay shaded and never dry out properly in winter.
