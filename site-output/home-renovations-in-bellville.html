01The area

Bellville, as we find it

Bellville is mostly family housing built between the 1950s and 1980s, and most of it has been added to at least once. A carport became a room, a flat roof went over a stoep, a second bathroom appeared. Those additions are where the problems sit now.

We work Bellville from Goodwood, which is a short drive, so a site visit costs you nothing and a return trip is not a production. Renovations, waterproofing, painting, plastering and repairs, one crew and one written scope.

03What fails here

The Bellville problems we get called for

These come up more often in this area than in the city as a whole.

  • Rain driven onto north-facing walls finds old cracks and works into the plaster.
  • Flat-roofed stoep and garage additions leak at the parapet, not in the middle.
  • Extensions joined to the original house without a proper flashing let water into both.
