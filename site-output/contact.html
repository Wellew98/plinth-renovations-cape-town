What to have ready when you call

You do not need plans or a specification. It helps if you can tell us the age of the house, what the problem looks like and when it started, and whether the work has been attempted before. If you have photos, send them. If the ceiling is open or the tiles are off, that is the best time for us to see it.

For bigger jobs, we will book a site visit at a time that suits you and walk the whole property rather than only the room you mentioned. Most surprises in a renovation are two rooms away from the symptom.
