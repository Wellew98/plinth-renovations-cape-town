01Waterproofing

Where Cape Town roofs leak

Waterproofing in Cape Town starts with finding where water enters, not where the stain appears. The north-westerly front pushes rain sideways across roofs and under tiles, so most leaks sit at a junction, not mid-roof.

It fails in two ways: the membrane reaches the end of its life, which is predictable, or the detail was wrong from the start. No primer, a fall running back towards the wall, a flashing sealed with silicone instead of metal. We read moisture through the plaster, hose test where needed, and specify for the substrate.
