01Waterproofing Durbanville

Waterproofing in Durbanville

Waterproofing in Durbanville is mostly about wind-driven rain on the western and northern faces, and about walls that stay damp because water sits against them. Older properties in the area often have parapets and box gutters detailed before the materials we use now, and newer estates have flat roofs and balconies with the same junction problems.

We work across Durbanville from Goodwood, trace the entry point, and specify for the substrate. Roofs, parapets, balconies and damp wall bases are all handled by the same team.
