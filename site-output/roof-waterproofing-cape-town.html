01Roof Waterproofing

What roof waterproofing involves

Roof waterproofing in Cape Town is a specification decision before it is a labour one. The same house can need a torch-on membrane on a flat concrete slab, an acrylic coating on fibre cement, and lead flashings at the chimney, because each surface behaves differently in sideways rain.

We identify the roof type and pitch, then choose a system that suits both. Flat roofs need fall correction before membrane; tile and slate roofs need flashings at valleys, not a coating over the tiles.

How long roof waterproofing lasts

SystemRealistic life
Torch-on membrane, correctly detailedTen to fifteen years before recoating
Liquid applied systemFive to ten years, dependent on prep and exposure
Acrylic coatingThree to five years, and it is a coating not a repair

Life depends on preparation, not product. Membrane on a dirty, wet surface fails early.

How we work

1

1. Inspect the roof

Surface, pitch, drainage, and every junction that interrupts it.

2

2. Check the ceiling

Where water shows inside tells us which part of the roof is lying.

3

3. Prepare

Strip failed material, correct falls, prime the substrate.

4

4. Detail and cover

Upstands and flashings first, then the field, then a water test.

  • Falls before membrane: Standing water is a design fault, not a coating fault.
  • Metal, not silicone: Flashings are replaced in metal where they should be.
  • Ten-year workmanship view: We specify for the second decade, not the first winter.
