Privacy policy

This policy explains what we do with the information you give us. It follows the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

Only what you send us: your name, phone number, email address, the address of the property, and what you tell us about the work. If you call, we may keep a note of the conversation so the quote is accurate.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry, to quote, to do the work, and to keep a record of what was agreed. We do not sell your information and we do not add you to a mailing list you did not ask for.

Who sees it

Us, and the people working on your job. Our website and form provider process the submission on our behalf. We do not pass your details to other contractors or lead buyers.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become jobs are kept only as long as needed to answer you. Job records are kept for as long as we may need them for accounting and for standing behind our work.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Call 066 150 5770 and ask.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising cookies. If we add call or visit analytics later, this page will say what it does and what it stores.
